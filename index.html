CenFit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

CenFit is a private app for food, recipes, health and the gym, used by people its owner invites. This page explains what CenFit stores, why, who else receives it and how to delete it. CenFit has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and never sells data.

Who is responsible

CenFit is run by Adam Nasli, a private individual. For any question or request about your data, write to adam.nasli95@gmail.com.

What CenFit stores

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored only in hashed form).
  • Profile: your name, sex, height and birth year, used for calorie and protein targets and health estimates. Your list of foods you don't eat.
  • Food diary: what you eat and drink, with calories and macros, and the foods you save.
  • Health: weight, blood pressure and steps, entered by you or read from Apple Health with your permission. Your routine (supplements, medicines, habits) and what you ticked off.
  • Gym: your workouts, visits and progress.
  • Photos: recipe photos and, if you take them, body progress photos.
  • Shared with your partner: recipes, the meal plan, the shopping list and what's at home belong to the two of you together.
  • AI usage: when you use an AI feature, the date, the kind of request and its estimated cost, so the owner can see what the AI costs.

Who can see it

  • You.
  • Your partner, if you join a couple in CenFit: your shared recipes, meal plan and shopping list. Your food diary, health records and progress photos only if you turn on sharing in Profile → Sharing & privacy. Your partner can change your food diary only if you allow that there too.
  • The app's owner can see each person's AI usage and cost, and invited email addresses. Like any app owner with access to its server, the owner can technically access stored data, but uses it only to run and fix the app.

Apple Health

With your permission, CenFit reads your weight, blood pressure and steps from Apple Health. It never writes to Apple Health. Health data is used only to show your own progress in CenFit (and your partner's view, if you share it). It is never used for advertising, never sold and never shared with anyone else.

Camera, microphone and speech

The camera is used only when you take a photo. The microphone and speech recognition are used only while you tap the microphone to say what you ate or what to change; Apple's speech recognition turns your words into text. Audio is not stored.

Services CenFit uses

  • Supabase stores your account and data, and the photos, in Ireland (EU), encrypted in transit.
  • Google Gemini (an AI service) receives the text, photos or recipe you send when you use an AI feature, such as estimating what you ate or reading a recipe. It is the paid Gemini API, under which Google does not use the content to train its models.
  • USDA FoodData Central receives only food names, to look up calories.
  • Open Food Facts receives product names or barcodes when you compare products.
  • Apple delivers notifications (for example a nudge from your partner) and provides the App Store and TestFlight.

None of these services receives your data for advertising.

How long data is kept

Your data is kept while you have an account. Anything you delete in the app is deleted from the server. Videos you send for recipe import are deleted right after they're read.

Deleting your account

In CenFit, open Profile → Delete my account and confirm. Your account and your personal data are deleted for good: profile, food diary, health records, gym history, routine, progress photos, saved foods and AI usage. Recipes, the meal plan and the shopping list you share with a partner stay for them; if you have no partner, they are deleted too. You can also ask for deletion by email.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or its deletion at any time by writing to the email address above. If you are in the EU, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

CenFit is not meant for children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
